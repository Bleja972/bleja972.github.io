How to play

  1. Read the problem and look at the model.
  2. Work it out, then write or choose your answer.
  3. Use “Clear” if you want to enter your answer again.
  4. Select “Check”, then move to the next problem.

Each checked answer counts once towards your score. You can clear your answer before checking. Your final summary shows your score and success percentage.
